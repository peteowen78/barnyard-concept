How it works

Three steps. One of them is ours.

Call us

Ring 01606 871445 with your order and postcode. Slots book up quickly, so call in good time.

Pay up front

Orders are paid in full before they leave the shop, delivery charge included.

We turn up

Our own team brings it to you in the Barnyard trailer and unloads it by pallet truck.

Yard orders

Share a yard? Share a drop.

Club together with others on your yard for one delivery. The delivery charge is paid with the first order, and each person's order needs to meet the minimum.

The small print, in plain English

Help us help you.

A few things that keep deliveries quick and our team safe.

  • Hard ground onlyWe deliver to an area of hardstanding. The trailer won't be driven through fields or muddy areas.
  • Room for the trailerYour delivery spot needs to be reachable by our large box trailer and vehicle.
  • Unloaded, not carriedItems come off by pallet truck. We can't walk each item to where you'd like it stored.
  • Animals awayLivestock must not be loose in the delivery area.
  • If we can't deliverIf delivery fails despite reasonable attempts, a re-delivery charge applies.
  • Left unattendedWe can't accept responsibility for loss or damage to items left in a designated area unattended.
