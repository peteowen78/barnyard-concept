Good to know

Before you come.

Paw Prints loyalty clubGift cards
How long is a session?
30 minutes, including the last 5 minutes to gather everyone up and leave the play area ready for the next guests.
Who looks after my dog?
You do. Our team is on hand to help, but you stay with your dog and are responsible for them throughout.
Is a private session completely private?
No other dogs come into the play area, but the dog wash stays open, so there may be other dogs in the building.
Are any dogs not allowed?
Because of insurance restrictions, we can't accept dogs covered by the Dangerous Dogs Act, including XL Bullies.
When are you open?
Dog Den hours are slightly different from the shop's. Check the booking page for today's times.
