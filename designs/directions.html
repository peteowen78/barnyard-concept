Three brand directions

Same name, three different personalities. Each board shows a logo idea, the colour palette, and how the top of the homepage would look and sound. These are rough sketches for choosing a direction, not finished designs.

Proper supplies for every animal you keep.

Feed, bedding and kit for horses, dogs, hens and everything in between. Family run, open seven days, five minutes from Northwich.

Order for deliveryFind the shop

The idea

A traditional country merchant, updated. Looks like a stamped feed sack: bold slab lettering, deep green, cream and rust. Type is Besley with Work Sans.

Voice

Knowledgeable and plain-spoken. "Ask us, we'll know."

Why it works

Credible to horse owners and smallholders, who spend the most, while still warm enough for pet owners. Nothing like Pets at Home. The Dog Den can sit alongside as a livelier sub-brand.

Risk

Could feel a touch serious for the ball-pit side of the business.

Muddy paws welcome.

The friendly local shop for dogs, horses, hens and humans. Bring the dog in, there's a ball pit next door.

Book the Dog DenWhat we stock

The idea

Keeps the mustard yellow people already know, but grows it up. Chunky, friendly lettering and bold flat colour replace the cartoon animals. Type is Bricolage Grotesque with DM Sans.

Voice

Cheerful and a bit cheeky. Talks like the team behind the till.

Why it works

The smallest leap for the owners, so the easiest sell. Great on social media and for the Dog Den.

Risk

Leans towards dogs and pets. Serious equestrian customers may feel less spoken to.

Explore equestrianVisit us →

The idea

A premium country-lifestyle look: quiet, elegant, lots of space. Navy, stone and brass with a fine serif. Type is Cormorant Garamond with Jost.

Voice

Calm and assured. Says less, means it.

Why it works

The biggest visual transformation, so the most dramatic before-and-after for a pitch.

Risk

Too posh for a shop that sells pigeon feed and bulk bedding at £6 a bag. May put off everyday customers and the owners themselves.
